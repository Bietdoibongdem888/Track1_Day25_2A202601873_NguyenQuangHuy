## Decision

The selected commercial metric is **Hybrid**: platform/governance access plus measured usage. The official pricing tab models the pure usage leg at **$1.75 per completed autonomous job**, while the commercial recommendation keeps access and usage distinct during pilot discovery.

Direct P-015 outcome pricing is not yet defensible because attribution is 2/10, autonomy is 1/10, the local controlled evaluation has only 5 cases, and 4/5 cases require human review. The 80.0% commercial package-completion result is not silently treated as autonomous containment or customer outcome evidence.

## Source and validation notes

- Intercom's public unit is one outcome/resolution; default escalation/failure handling is treated separately by its published policy.
- Zendesk's public page gives a starting resolution price; the page is not an exact P-015 cost or willingness-to-pay benchmark.
- The two benchmark rows are recorded in the official workbook at 3_Value_Metric!A26:D27.
- No customer willingness-to-pay, production resolution volume or analyst-time-saved measurement is claimed.
